ONE PORT. MANY LIVES.

Where Stories
Cross

Every story unfolds along a different path, yet each remains bound to the fate of Breakwater Port. From noble halls to forgotten streets, from ancient foundations to distant frontiers, their paths may cross, their consequences may linger, and their echoes may reach further than anyone imagined.

A city of merchants, old stone and restless tides. Wealth arrives daily. So do secrets.
Behind its prosperous harbors, the streets tell another story. From crowded marketplaces to impoverished slums, fortunes are made, lives are lost, and survival often comes at a price.
The Church of the Everburning Flame stands among the city’s most influential institutions, its presence woven into the lives of those who call Breakwater Port home.

Power is inherited, contested and defended.
The great houses of Breakwater Port shape the city’s fortunes through alliances, rivalries and carefully guarded secrets. Their influence stretches far beyond their mansions, reaching into commerce, politics and the lives of ordinary citizens.

Breakwater Port stands upon the remains of its own forgotten past. Beneath its streets lie the foundations of cities that came before.
Beyond the city, remnants of earlier ages lie scattered across the wilderness. Abandoned settlements, forgotten villages, ancient tombs and ruined temples stand as silent witnesses to histories long since lost.

Beyond the familiar streets and sheltered harbors lies a world less easily governed.
Untamed wilderness, distant settlements and forgotten places await those willing to venture beyond the known.
Some seek fortune. Others seek answers. Not all return with what they came to find.

Some secrets are buried. Some are hidden behind thick walls. Others are surrounded by water. Beyond the familiar shores of Breakwater Port lies an island shrouded in secrecy. Its existence is known. Its purpose is not. Yet the lives of those who find themselves drawn toward it will never be the same.

One city, a lifetime of stories

The first tale follows Sebastian Valecourt, his story marks the beginning of a larger narrative. One that set the fate of unsuspecting souls in motion throughout the streets of Breakwater Port and beyond.

NEWSLETTER AND UPDATES →

From bustling harbors and ancient stonework to the heraldry of powerful houses, the visual identity of Breakwater Port reflects a world of contrasts. Public grandeur conceals private tensions, while every street and forgotten corner offers another glimpse into the lives of its inhabitants.

Sebastian Valecourt appearance reflects his upbringing: confidence, refinement and a certain ease in the world around him. But a character is more than what the world sees. Every expression, gesture and detail offers a glimpse of the person beneath the surface. Some things are better left for the story itself to reveal.

For some, it is a city of opportunity and influence. For others, a place of hardship, uncertainty and survival. Though their journeys may lead far beyond its shores, the choices they make and the lives they touch remain connected to Breakwater Port. Every perspective reveals another piece of a much larger story.
